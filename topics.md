## How the topics fit together

{% include figures/topic-flow.html %}

*Beyond income* and *Wealth and household finances* measure: they describe inequalities in living conditions and in what households own and owe. *Beyond averages* provides the statistical tools to follow these inequalities over time and to link them to economic shocks. *Inequality and the economy* uses this evidence in economic models, to test policies before they are applied. *Place, opportunity and social ties* adds the geographic and social dimension: the same household can face very different opportunities depending on where it lives.

## Themes across topics

Some questions cut across several topics: climate and energy; housing; expectations and perceptions; childhood and generations; data and methods; and the main areas of EU policy (monetary, fiscal, cohesion and social policy). Each topic page lists the publications and events that concern it, and a publication can belong to more than one topic.
