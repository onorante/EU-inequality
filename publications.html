Types of publication

  • Policy briefs: short, non-technical summaries of a result and of what it means for policy. The best starting point for non-specialists.
  • Working papers: full research papers, before or alongside publication in scientific journals.
  • Technical reports: detailed documentation of methods and data.
  • Handbook chapters: chapters of the handbook New methodologies for assessing inequalities.
  • Data notes: descriptions of new datasets and indicators.
  • Presentations: slides from seminars and events.

Each publication has its own page, with a summary in plain words, the abstract, links to the full text and to the code, and a suggested citation.

Handbook

A handbook, New methodologies for assessing inequalities, will bring together the main results: new data and measurement approaches, the links between inequality and economic and social outcomes, and recommendations for policy. It is expected in 2028; its chapters will be listed here.
