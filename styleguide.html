Type

One family, Source Sans 3, in three weights: regular for text, semibold for headings and bold for emphasis.

Layout options

Set once in _config.yml under layout_options, or for a single page under layout_options in its front matter.

OptionValuesWhat it changes
widthnarrow, standard, wideMaximum width of every page
headerinline, centeredLogo and menu side by side, or centred one above the other
sectionssplit, stackedSection title beside its items, or above them with items in a grid
color_schemelight, autoAlways white, or dark when the visitor's device is in dark mode
