sections:
  - id: indicators
    title: Indicators
    intro: The measures used most often on this site.
    items:
      - title: Gini coefficient
        text: One number for a whole distribution, from 0, when everyone has the same, to 1, when one person has everything.
      - title: Income shares
        text: The share of total income held by a group, such as the poorest half or the richest tenth.
      - title: Income quintile share ratio (S80/S20)
        text: The total income of the richest fifth of the population divided by that of the poorest fifth.
      - title: At-risk-of-poverty rate
        text: The share of people whose equivalised disposable income is below 60% of the national median.
      - title: Wealth shares
        text: The same idea applied to net wealth, that is, assets minus debts.

  - id: datasets
    title: Datasets
    intro: Main sources, with links to the institutions that publish them. Access conditions differ; some microdata require an approved research proposal.
    items:
      - title: EU-SILC
        url: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/microdata/european-union-statistics-on-income-and-living-conditions
        text: EU Statistics on Income and Living Conditions. Household income, poverty and living conditions, every year. Eurostat.
      - title: Household Finance and Consumption Survey
        url: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/hfcs
        text: Household wealth, debt and consumption, collected in survey waves, with extra coverage of the wealthiest households. European Central Bank.
      - title: Distributional Wealth Accounts
        url: https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/DWA/data-information
        text: How household wealth is distributed, consistent with the national accounts. European Central Bank.
      - title: Household Budget Survey
        url: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/household-budget-surveys
        text: What households spend their money on. Eurostat.
      - title: EU Labour Force Survey
        url: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/microdata/european-union-labour-force-survey
        text: Employment, unemployment and hours worked. Eurostat.
      - title: Structure of Earnings Survey
        url: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/microdata/structure-of-earnings-survey
        text: Wages and how they differ across employees and firms. Eurostat.
      - title: EUROMOD
        url: https://euromod-web.jrc.ec.europa.eu
        text: The tax-benefit microsimulation model for the EU, used to see how taxes and benefits change household incomes. European Commission, Joint Research Centre.
      - title: LIS and LWS databases
        url: https://www.lisdatacenter.org/
        text: Income and wealth microdata harmonised across countries, for international comparison. LIS Cross-National Data Center.
      - title: GISCO
        url: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/gisco
        text: Maps and geographic data, including the NUTS regions used for regional statistics. Eurostat.
