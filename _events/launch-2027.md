The launch opens the research phase. A closed expert session in the morning, where the research teams present their work plans and discuss synergies across topics, is followed by a public afternoon with opening remarks, a keynote lecture, presentations of the five research topics, and a panel on the future of inequality measurement for EU policy.

The programme, the speakers and the registration details will be published here once confirmed.
